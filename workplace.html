Our Workplace

The work behind every requirement.

Every garment passes through a series of practical steps, from fabric selection and cutting to stitching, finishing and packing. Explore the processes and working environments behind garment production.

01 / MATERIALS

Fabric Storage

Fabric is stored and organised before it moves into production. Suitable material selection is an important part of meeting garment requirements.

02 / PREPARATION

Fabric Cutting

Fabric layers are prepared and cut into garment components according to the required patterns and measurements.

03 / CONSTRUCTION

Stitching Unit

Cut components are assembled through stitching, bringing the garment design together piece by piece.

04 / ASSEMBLY

Garment Assembly

Individual garment components are joined and prepared for the next stages of production.

05 / FINISHING

Finishing & Pressing

Garments are pressed and finished to help achieve a neat appearance before final inspection.

06 / REVIEW

Quality Review

Garments can be reviewed for appearance, measurements, stitching and finishing details according to the applicable requirements.

07 / READY FOR DISPATCH

Finished Garments & Storage

Finished garments are organised and prepared for packing, storage and onward dispatch.
