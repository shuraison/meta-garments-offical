About Us

Get to Know Meta Garments.

Meta Garments is an Indian garment business focused on supplying shirts to wholesalers and businesses serving the US market. We aim to build dependable supply relationships through clear communication, competitive pricing, and an understanding of each client's requirements.

Our Three Major Priorities

01 / PRIORITY

Competitive Pricing

We aim to offer competitive rates while meeting agreed specifications and quality requirements. Pricing depends on the fabric, product details, order quantity, and commercial terms.

02 / PRIORITY

Predictable and Planned Supply

We work with clients to understand required quantities, product specifications, and preferred schedules, then discuss practical production and supply arrangements.

03 / PRIORITY

Long-Term Partnerships

Our objective is to develop lasting, contract-based relationships founded on clear communication, mutual understanding, and agreed expectations.

Customisation Around Your Requirements

Clients can share reference designs or samples, along with their preferred fabrics, colours, collars, cuffs, and other details. We discuss the requirements and assess what is feasible before proceeding.

Building Something That Lasts

We want to build long-term working relationships with wholesalers who value dependable communication and carefully planned supply arrangements. We recognise that trust is earned through actions and consistency.
