About Us

Get to know Meta Garments.

Meta Garments is an Indian garment business focused on supplying shirts to wholesalers and businesses serving the US market. We believe that a strong business relationship is built on competitive pricing, dependable supply planning, and a clear understanding of our clients' requirements.

Our Three Major Priorities

1. Competitive Pricing

We understand that pricing plays an important role in the success of a wholesale business. Our aim is to offer competitive rates while meeting agreed specifications and quality requirements. Depending on the product, fabric, order quantity, and commercial terms, we aim to offer prices up to 20% below comparable market rates on qualifying orders.

2. Predictable and Planned Supply

We recognise that wholesalers need to plan their purchases around customer demand, inventory, and business commitments. Our goal is to understand each client's required quantities, product specifications, and preferred schedules, then work towards practical production and supply arrangements.

3. Long-Term Business Partnerships

Our primary objective is to establish lasting, contract-based relationships rather than focus solely on one-time transactions. We want to understand the businesses we work with and develop supply arrangements that can grow alongside them through clear communication and mutually agreed expectations.

Customisation Around Your Requirements

We welcome discussions about customised shirts, including fabric selection, colours, collars, cuffs, and other design details. Clients can share a reference design or sample so we can understand their expectations and assess the feasibility of their requirements.

Our Approach to Business

We value open discussions, clear specifications, realistic planning, and mutually agreed terms. We believe a dependable supplier should understand a client's requirements before making commitments.

Building Something That Lasts

Our objective extends beyond supplying individual orders. We want to develop lasting relationships with wholesalers who value competitive pricing, predictable supply planning, and a supplier willing to understand their needs. We recognise that trust must be earned through actions and consistency over time.

Our objective is straightforward: to become a dependable shirt supply partner that your business can build a long-term relationship with.
